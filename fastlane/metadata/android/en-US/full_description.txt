Tasks is a native Android task manager that works offline and can synchronize with a CalDAV server of your choice. No application account or Google Play Services is required.

Organize work and personal tasks with lists, subtasks, notes, stars, tags, and priorities. See every list in an undated-first chronological summary, or select My order, hold a task, and drag it into your preferred arrangement.

Create daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, and custom repeating tasks with start dates, times, intervals, weekdays, and end-date or occurrence limits. Set separate reminders and deadlines, and use the home-screen widget to view, add, open, and complete tasks. Resize the widget and scroll through the full collection, or switch between All, Starred, and individual lists within it.

Choose light, shaded dark, AMOLED pure black, or system appearance, optional wallpaper colors, and individual tab colors, icons, or emoji. Hide All and Starred independently. Choose whether long-press reorders tasks or opens a menu at your finger. Add and edit subtasks inline. Checked subtasks stay crossed out until the parent is completed; checking every subtask completes the family. Repeating tasks create fresh unchecked subtasks. Leading tabs stay left-aligned; later selections center in sync with page swipes. Task reordering uses a soft lift and release transition. Restore archived tasks from Settings.

Synchronization is optional. Select synchronization while using the app, only while charging, or manually, with an optional Wi-Fi restriction. There are no ads, analytics, or periodic widget polling.

Task tags, priorities, recurrence, archive state, and manual order synchronize through CalDAV. Archive state is an app-specific property that other clients may ignore. Tab appearance and sort preferences stay on each device. HTTPS protects synchronization in transit; task content is not end-to-end encrypted by this application.

The application is under active development. Physical Android 17 and CalyxOS testing, real-server interoperability, and measured battery validation are ongoing.
